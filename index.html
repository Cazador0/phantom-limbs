MEET PHANTOM LIMBS

Dallas band Phantom Limbs brings together an eclectic mix of musical influences and backgrounds, blending elements of rock, funk, R&B and beyond.

Keyboardist/guitarist Joren Cain and guitarist Danny Enriquez, the principal songwriters behind beloved DFW jazz group Lovers & Lunatics (2018–2026), have joined forces with three accomplished musicians: vocalist Tiffany Baucum, drummer Kei Tabuchi and bassist Robert Tracy.

Together, they create a sound shaped by distinct perspectives, unexpected connections and a shared appetite for musical exploration.
